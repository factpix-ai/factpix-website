Refund Policy

Last updated: June 26, 2025

We want you to be completely satisfied with FactPix. If you are not happy with your purchase, we're here to help. Please read our refund policy carefully.

1. Monthly Subscriptions

If you subscribed to a monthly plan and are not satisfied, you may request a full refund within 7 days of your initial subscription purchase. To request a refund, contact us at factpix.ai@gmail.com with your WhatsApp number and reason for the refund.

Refund requests made after the 7-day window will not be eligible for a refund, but you may cancel your subscription at any time to prevent future charges. Your subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing period.

2. One-Time Credit Packs

One-time credit packs (Starter and Pro Pack) are eligible for a full refund within 48 hours of purchase, provided that fewer than 3 credits from the pack have been used.

Once more than 3 credits have been used, the pack is considered partially consumed and is no longer eligible for a refund.

3. Free Tier

The free tier is provided at no cost and is therefore not eligible for any refund.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us via:

  • Email: factpix.ai@gmail.com
  • Include your WhatsApp phone number associated with your account
  • Include the date of purchase and the reason for your refund request

We aim to process all refund requests within 3–5 business days. Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at the time of purchase.

5. Payment Processing

All payments are processed by Paddle, our authorized Merchant of Record. Refunds will be processed through Paddle and may take 5–10 business days to appear on your statement depending on your bank or card issuer.

6. Exceptions

We reserve the right to refuse a refund in the following circumstances:

  • The refund request is made outside the eligible time window
  • There is evidence of abuse of the refund policy (e.g., repeated refund requests)
  • The account has been suspended due to violation of our Terms of Service

7. Service Disruptions

In the unlikely event of a significant service disruption lasting more than 24 hours that prevents you from using your paid credits, we will provide a proportional credit extension or refund at our discretion.
