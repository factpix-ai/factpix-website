Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to FactPix ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our WhatsApp bot service.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to provide our service:

WhatsApp Phone Number: We collect your WhatsApp phone number to identify your account, track your usage credits, and send you results.

Images Submitted for Analysis: Images you send to FactPix are processed in real-time for AI detection analysis. We do not store, retain, or share your images. They are permanently deleted immediately after analysis.

Usage Data: We track the number of checks you have used to enforce the free tier limits and manage paid subscriptions.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to: provide the image analysis service, manage your account and credits, process payments (via Lemon Squeezy), and improve our service quality.

4. Data Retention

Your phone number and credit balance are stored securely in our database as long as your account is active. Images are never stored — they are processed and discarded immediately.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services: Hive Moderation for AI image analysis, 360Dialog for WhatsApp Business API connectivity, and Lemon Squeezy for payment processing. Each of these services has their own privacy policies.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at factpix.ai@gmail.com.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: factpix.ai@gmail.com
